Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Your privacy is fundamentally important to us. This policy details how our system processes user metadata and ensures a secure chatting environment.

1. Information We Collect

We prioritize minimal data collection. We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal identity documents. The infrastructure briefly tracks:

  • User-Provided Attributes: The temporary custom username, gender, and regional preference input during session initiation.
  • Session Telemetry: Connection indicators, socket instances, and temporary masking identifiers required to securely match peers.

2. Video & Audio Stream Architecture

Media data transmission is completely encrypted and processed natively via **Peer-to-Peer architecture (WebRTC technology)**. Video streams and text messages are handled directly from browser to browser. No media streams are ever captured, inspected, intercepted, or saved on our servers.

3. Incident Logging & Ban Data

To enforce our platform's safety guardrails, if a user is explicitly flagged for pornography or harassment, their identifier is securely committed to our backend SQLite database storage alongside a timestamp expiration limit. This data is exclusively utilized to restrict network access for the duration of the ban enforcement window.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not commercialize, sell, or trade any transactional session data to advertising networks. Data access is strictly restricted unless lawfully commanded under legal warrants issued by cyber police authorities.
